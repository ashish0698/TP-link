OUR STORY

Founded by two people who wanted IT support done properly

TP Link Traders was founded and is run by Damodar Dumre and Jhabilal Dumre, out of a shop on Newroad, Pokhara. What started as a counter for computer sales grew into a full IT partner for the region — supplying laptops, desktops, CCTV cameras, networking equipment and accessories for homes, offices, schools and businesses across Gandaki.

Alongside sales, our technicians handle installation, repair, AMC and on-site support, so the technology we sell keeps working long after it leaves the shop.
